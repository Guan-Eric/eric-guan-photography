# What You Need To Start A Real Estate Photo Business

Notes from [Eli Jones — Aug 2024](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=placeholder) (Norman & Young / Dallas–Fort Worth pricing as a mid-market reference).

Use this for **package ideas** and **what to buy next**. Prices and gear SKUs are illustrative — adjust for your market (e.g. Montréal / GTA).

---

## Packages to offer (Norman & Young model)

### Tier packages (drive average order value)

| Package | Rough price (DFW 2024) | What’s in it |
|---|---|---|
| **Base** | ~$195 | ~25 photos · drone photos · basic floor plan + room measurements (CubiCasa) |
| **Pro** | ~$365 | More photos · same floor plan · **Zillow 3D tour** |
| **Plus** | ~$825 | More media · **Matterport-style 3D tour** · 3D floor plan · CubiCasa floor plan · ~12 drone photos · **60s video** · virtual twilight |

**Land / lot** (Texas-oriented; adapt if you shoot vacant land):

- Base lot listing  
- Land listing (more photos)  
- Plus land (video + 360 drone tour, etc.)

### A-la-carte (for agents who won’t buy a bundle)

- Photo packs: large (~60) → mid → small (~10–15, often rentals / lower commission)  
- **20/20/20** builder package: before · during · after (photos + reels)  
- Standalone drone photos  
- Video: **Reels** (vertical, prefer iPhone) · horizontal “regular” video  
- Twilight (real or virtual)  
- Virtual staging (empty room → editor adds furniture)

### Package design tips from the video

1. Prefer **named packages** over pure à-la-carte — raises average order value.  
2. Bundle **floor plans into every package** (CubiCasa preferred-photographer perks: faster turnaround, ~25% off when included in shoot price). Use “floor plan included on every shoot” as a sales hook.  
3. Keep Zillow 3D for **ranking bump**; Matterport (or equivalent) for **higher-quality walkthrough**. Offering both in different tiers is intentional.  
4. Virtual twilight / virtual staging = **no new camera gear** — daylight stills + editor.

## Gear: start lean, then upgrade

### Minimum to start (photos only)

You only need **four things**:

1. Tripod (any decent budget tripod)  
2. Camera body (anything ~last 10 years often works)  
3. **Wide lens** (this matters more than the body)  
   - Crop sensor: ~**10–18 mm** or **11–22 mm**  
   - Full frame: ~**16–35 mm** (widest end ~16 mm)  
4. Memory card  

Skip video/drone/360 until clients are paying you.

### Budget kit → what to add next (priority order)

| Priority | Buy next | Unlocks |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | You’re shooting photos already | Cash flow |
| 2 | **Phone app: CubiCasa** (no gear) | Floor plans on every package |
| 3 | **Drone** (used Mini 3 / similar OK; Facebook Marketplace / Craigslist) | Base package with aerials |
| 4 | **360 camera** (e.g. Insta360 X3) | Fast Zillow / Matterport-style tours (phone works but is slow) |
| 5 | **Stabilizer** for video — DJI Osmo Mobile (phone) or Ronin-SC (camera) | 60s listing video + reels |
| 6 | Pro body/tripod/gimbal upgrade | Battery life, comfort on multi-shoot days, slightly better video — **photos look the same** |

### Pro kit vs budget kit (when to upgrade)

Upgrade when you’re doing **many shoots per day**, not because photos look bad:

- Better tripod + head  
- Larger body / better battery life  
- Gimbal that holds a heavier camera  

Photos stay roughly the same; daily usability and client perception improve.

### Video note

- **Reels / vertical**: shoot on **iPhone** (even if you own a Ronin + camera for horizontal). Auto exposure handles indoor↔outdoor better.  
- Horizontal marketing video: camera + gimbal when ready.  
- Phone alone can start Matterport / Zillow tours; 360 camera is a speed/quality upgrade.

## Software (business ops)

| Tool | Role | Pricing model called out |
|---|---|---|
| **Spiro** (spiro.media) | Booking/order page, schedule, delivery, property websites | No base fee in the pitch — **pay per delivered project** (~$5/project mentioned) |
| **CubiCasa** | Phone floor plans (+ 3D renders, etc.) | Preferred program if floor plans are included in every shoot |
| Zillow 3D app | Free tours + listing ranking bump | Free |
| Matterport (or peer) | Premium dollhouse / walkthrough | Phone or 360 |

Studiofront already covers booking → gallery → pay unlock → listing site. Use this list to decide **what media products** to put in your packages, not necessarily which SaaS to switch to.
