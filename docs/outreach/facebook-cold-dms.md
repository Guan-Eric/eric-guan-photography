## Before you send

1. Like 2-3 recent **listing** posts on their page.
2. Swap in the `[HOOK]` line (one specific post or photo). Do not invent a city or specialty.
3. **One DM.** One bump 48-72h later max, then stop.
4. If they say pass, don't follow up again.

### Voice rules

- 4-7 sentences. Sounds like a person, not a landing page.
- Personal hook first, offer second, link third.
- Say the price and the caps.
- No bullet lists in the DM. No em dashes.

### Bump (48-72h, once)

Hey, bumping this in case it got buried. Lifetime seats are limited and I'm closing the private round soon. 60s walkthrough if easier than the page: https://studiofront.ca/demo
