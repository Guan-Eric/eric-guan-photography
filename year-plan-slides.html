Real Estate Launch Plan02
GearWeek 0 purchase

Buy this wide-angle lens

Primary recommendation

Sigma 10–18mm f/2.8 DC DN

Sony E-mount · APS-C · Contemporary

  • 10mm ≈ 15mm full-frame — best for tight rooms
  • Light and easy for all-day listing shoots
  • Shoot interiors around f/7.1–f/9
  • Budget: ~$600–650 new · ~$450–550 used
Runner-up

Tamron 11–20mm f/2.8

Slightly less wide, more reach + weather sealing.

Do not buy yet
  • Gimbal
  • Drone
  • Second camera body
  • Complex lighting kits

Real Estate Launch Plan04
Month 0Weeks 1–2

Setup before you message anyone

Camera presets
  • RAW always
  • f/7.1–f/9
  • ISO 100–400
  • WB Auto or Kelvin ~4000–5000
  • Bracket ±1 or ±2 EV for bright windows
  • Use grid — keep verticals straight
Practice goal

Shoot 3 homes free

Your place, family/friend home, or open house with permission.

  • Edit bright, clean, true color
  • Straight verticals on every frame
  • Deliver 15–30 images per home
  • Replace all stock photos on your site

Real Estate Launch Plan10
Month 1Calls + free jobs

10-minute call agenda

  • Show 6 best photos on your phone
  • Explain: book → shoot 45–90 min → deliver 24–48h
  • Ask what they hate about current photographers
  • Ask when their next listing is
  • Offer one discounted shoot + review after
Discount rules
  • One discounted property per agent max
  • Call it an “intro rate” in writing
  • Get permission to use photos
  • Ask for a referral after delivery

Real Estate Launch Plan12
Month 2On-site

Shoot-day checklist

  • Arrive 5–10 min early
  • Walk the property once
  • Lights on, TV off, fans off
  • Hide trash, cords, remotes, magnets
  • Exterior first if light is good
  • Verticals straight every frame
  • Consistent camera height
Month 2 weekly goals
  • 2–4 paid or discounted shoots
  • 5 new agent outreaches / week
  • Collect 3 written testimonials
Real Estate Launch Plan13
Month 3Quality + soft video

Raise quality — keep video light

Do this
  • Add flash for dark rooms / twilight practice
  • Learn simple window pull / HDR blend
  • Raise prices ~10–20% after 8–10 jobs
  • Ask every happy agent for team intros
  • Attend 1 local realtor networking event
Video without a gimbal
  • 5–8 locked tripod clips
  • 20–40s vertical Reel
  • Stabilize in CapCut if needed
  • Charge +$50–100 at first
  • Only after photos are solid

Real Estate Launch Plan16
Months 10–12Stabilize

End-of-year goals

  • 4–8 shoots per month (more if going full-time)
  • 50%+ work from repeats / referrals
  • Market pricing — not intro pricing
  • Site shows only your real listings
  • Emergency kit: batteries, cards, cloths, rain cover
Decide with data
  • Agents ask for video often → used gimbal
  • Luxury work appears → twilight mastery
  • Calendar mostly full → raise 15–25%
  • Slow months → reactivate past agents

Admin: track mileage + gear · separate business account when regular · save ~25–30% for taxes · simple service agreement.
